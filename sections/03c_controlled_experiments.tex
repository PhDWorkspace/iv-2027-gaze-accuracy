\subsection{Controlled Experiments}
\label{sec:method_controlled}

Driving conditions co-occur, so the benchmark alone cannot tell which of
them affects gaze. We therefore designed a controlled study around
factors that occur in driving: viewing distance (near and distant
objects), position in the field of view (signs, mirrors, road ahead),
screen brightness and thus pupil size (sunlight and tunnels), indoor
versus outdoor recording (ambient infrared light), static fixation versus
smooth pursuit (moving traffic), and repetition over time
(Table~\ref{tab:factors}). The dataset comprises 216 recordings (2.9\,h)
from six participants, including both benchmark participants; not every
participant completed every protocol. Static targets are the 15 inner
corners of chessboard patterns on screens or printed boards. A QR code
identifies the protocol and target layout automatically, followed by
manual checks. In the pursuit protocol, a target moves along a predefined
trajectory. We evaluate the central 60\% of each fixation interval to
exclude gaze transitions.
