
\subsection{Controlled Experiments}
\label{sec:method_controlled}

We designed complementary controlled experiments to systematically
investigate factors relevant to driving: viewing distance, gaze
position and eccentricity, illumination and pupil size, indoor
versus outdoor conditions, smooth pursuit, and temporal stability
(Sec.~\ref{sec:controlled}).

Static references comprise the 15 inner corners of chessboard
patterns displayed on screens or printed boards at varying
distances. QR codes enable automatic identification of the
experimental protocol and target layout, followed by manual
verification. Smooth-pursuit experiments use predefined
trajectories at different speeds and directions. We evaluate
the central 60\% of each static fixation interval to exclude
gaze transitions.

The protocols combine standardized reference measurements
with systematic variation of driving-relevant conditions.
