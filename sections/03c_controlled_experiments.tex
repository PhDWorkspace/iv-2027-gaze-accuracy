\subsection{Controlled Experiments}
\label{sec:method_controlled}
\todo{intro: how controlled experiments isolate distance-dependent and other influencing factors}

The controlled dataset contains 216 recordings (2.9\,h) from six participants. Static targets are the 15 inner corners of 5$\times$3 chessboards shown on a screen with bright or dark background or printed, fixated sequentially in forward or reverse order at 1, 2 and 4\,m, indoors and outdoors. A smooth-pursuit protocol shows a target moving along a fixed route on the screen. P1 completed all protocols over several days (170 recordings), P2 the static and distance protocols (23 recordings); P3--P6 contributed 3--7 static screen recordings each, in bright and, except P4, dark conditions. For each target, the central 60\% of the fixation interval is analysed.
