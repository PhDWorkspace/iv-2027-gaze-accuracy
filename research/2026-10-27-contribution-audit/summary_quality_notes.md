# Summary quality: confirmed issues

**28 October 2026.** Findings from the contribution audit and targeted follow-up checks; this is not a complete factual audit of all 125 summaries. Most identified issues concern metadata, terminology or interpretation. The checks do not establish that the summaries generally misrepresent the papers' central methods or findings.

| Summary key | Type | Issue / clarification |
|---|---|---|
| `ehinger2019testBattery` | Attribution | Names Niehorster et al., 2018; the original paper is Ehinger et al., 2019. |
| `hu2022data` | Metadata | Infers incorrect publication details. The PDF identifies IEEE Transactions on Industrial Electronics, 69(2), February 2022. |
| `hu2022data` | Terminology | Treats CC/NSS as eye-tracker precision measures. They assess attention/saliency predictions, not measurement precision. |
| `hu2022data` | Overstatement | Calls VR ground truth “perfect.” Error-free gaze measurement is not established. |
| `salari2026pupilSize` | Missing qualification | Should explicitly distinguish Neon's apparent gaze shift from the absence of a statistically significant accuracy change in that experiment. |

These issues matter when citing evidence or positioning contributions, especially when interpreting measurement quality. They do not by themselves invalidate an entire summary. The current Jia, Barz and Foucher summaries already include important qualifications about passenger testing, evaluation splits and infrared interference.

**Status:** issues documented; summaries have not been corrected as part of this note. The contribution report uses the primary papers for the relevant distinctions.
